## Automatic speech recognition planning

Use this site to reason about ASR workflows before selecting a model, vendor, or deployment path.

Start with representative audio, measure transcription quality, estimate review load, and confirm privacy requirements before moving to production.

## Core checklist

1. Collect clean, noisy, accented, and overlapping speech samples.
2. Test word error rate, timestamps, punctuation, and speaker diarization.
3. Add domain vocabulary for names, acronyms, products, and technical phrases.
4. Define review rules for low-confidence words and sensitive content.
5. Validate exports for captions, search, QA, analytics, and records.
