## Overview

Automatic Speech Recognition is an informational and planning website for speech-to-text workflows. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit automatic-speech-recognition.homes.

## Information We May Collect

- Basic usage data such as pages viewed, approximate device type, browser type, and referral source.
- Information you send by email, including your email address and message content.
- Cookies or similar browser storage used for site preferences, analytics, abuse prevention, or locale behavior.

## Audio and Transcript Data

The public planner on this website does not upload, store, process, or transcribe your audio. Do not send sensitive recordings, transcripts, credentials, or regulated data by email unless a separate written agreement is in place.

## How Information Is Used

We use collected information to operate the website, understand aggregate usage, respond to messages, prevent abuse, and improve ASR planning resources.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate the website, or when required to comply with law, protect rights, prevent abuse, or maintain security.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No website or network transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy by posting a revised version on this page.
